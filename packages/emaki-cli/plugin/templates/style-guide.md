# Style guide (settled on the pilot; every story follows it)

## Look
- Board, palette, fonts, the accent's use (what matters), the success colour (what's right).
- Card style, tag style, connector style, chart style.

## Layout rules
- Words left (x 110, w ≈ 700); art right (x 880–1820, y 150–830); 40 px margins.
- Connectors end on edge centres; repeated cards one size; even spacing; column headers ≥ 40 px above items.

## Motion grammar
- Head arrives at −0.4 s; art builds in the order the voice names it; holds ≥ 1.5 s; camera push 1.00→1.02.

## Words
- Headlines ≤ 9 words; subs ≤ 20; punchlines 3–9; plain, direct, no hype; glossary terms.

## Sound
- Action tones only; a note per beat; the closing chord.
